Developments

A dated record of what has happened to the estate and around it.

Publications, external events the framework interlocks with, releases. Entries are added, never rewritten. RSS feed.

2026-09-15Publication
What the Signature Certifies: the consultation paper is published as record twenty.

NHS England is revising DCB0129 and DCB0160. The companion paper to the author’s consultation response is now open access: four asks, with the requirement-level drafting to implement them.

2026-09-12External
DSIT publishes an AI Risk Management Toolkit. It measures the machine, not the room.

The Department for Science, Innovation and Technology has released a risk toolkit for public-sector AI. Nine categories, all capability-side. Where the estate supplies the missing culture term.

2026-09-10External
The National Commission reports. Where the estate meets it.

The MHRA-hosted National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare has published its recommendations. Six of them describe gaps this estate already carries instruments for.

2026-08-29Publication
The Tenant’s Record published as record nineteen.

A working paper on tenancy and ownership: who the system serves when the system is not yours. Published open access, with a new Sovereignty page on the estate to carry the doctrine.

2026-08-21Publication
The Instrument Deck: all eight instruments in deployment order.

A single 38-page deck consolidating the estate’s eight instruments — Gate, Screen, Specify, Oversee, Trial, Audit, Measure, Record — in the sequence they are meant to be run.
