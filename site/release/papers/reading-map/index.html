The Heartbeat Framework: Reading Map

Estate document. The index to the estate at twenty-one records: three core papers, seven applied instruments, six extended works, two standing instruments, the instrument deck, two implementation papers, and the order to read them in.

Paul Blatherwick, RMN · doi:10.5281/zenodo.22994740

View on ZenodoDownload PDFAll records

Abstract

This record is the reading map of the Heartbeat Framework — the index to a twenty-one-document open-access estate on team psychology for the 24-hour organisation, extended to the mixed human–machine shift: three core papers, seven applied instruments (A0–A6), six extended works, two standing instruments, the instrument deck, and two implementation papers.

The map gives every document a one-line summary and its DOI, a role-based entry table — start here, depending on who you are: deployer, ward manager, overseer, buyer, safety-case signatory, records-system buyer, builder, assessor, keyworker, inspector — the order of operations, and the standing rules that travel with everything: lenses, not labels · machines report observations, humans assign meaning · no accountability without authority · every machine behaviour has a return address · signals, never targets.

New in v1.4: the estate grows by five. The Tenant’s Record (who the system serves when the system is not yours) and What the Signature Certifies (four asks for the revision of DCB0129 and DCB0160) join the extended series. The Instrument Deck takes its own section as the working layer — the estate’s worksheets bound in the order a deployment meets them. A new section, the implementation, indexes Learning Outside the Model (a frozen-weight personal agent built to the estate’s rules: governed memory, a record the agent may not read, executable refusals) and its companion note, When the Weights Can Move. Four new entry rows route safety-case signatories, anyone choosing or leaving a care records system, builders of agent memory and audit records, and those who want the worksheets rather than the argument. The map is re-set full-width on the page, and its Markdown source and stylesheet are deposited alongside the PDF.

All records are open access under CC BY 4.0 (Glass Ledger code MIT), collected in the Zenodo community heartbeat-framework. Field returns, corrections, and null results are actively welcomed — the series improves by versioned revision, and contradicting results are published with thanks.
