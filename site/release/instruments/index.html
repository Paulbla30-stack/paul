The instruments

Eight instruments, one sequence: assess at the gate, measure over time, record beyond amendment.

The papers argue; these pages do. Each instrument is a worksheet, checklist or protocol lifted from a published record, placed in the order a deployment actually meets it. Every one of them can be run on a real unit without new software. All eight are bound, in this order, in The Instrument Deck.

Stage 1 · Before01
Gate

The Readiness Gate

Ten observable pre-deployment checks and four red lines. The only instrument whose output can be: do not deploy.

The AI Interaction Risk Screen and Change-Over-Time Log

Assesses how a person's AI use is functioning in their life, domain by domain, on the axis that matters: displacement or supplementation.

Onboarding worksheet, shift card and oversight templates

The Floor Test capture sheets

Ninety days, no budget, no new software: baseline nine vitals, change one thing, read the result — and send it back, especially if it is null.

Stage 6 · Audit06

The Damping Audit

Maps and measures the discretion layer — where staff absorb, translate, correct, sense and escalate — and what a deployment does to it.

The AI Interaction Outcome Profile (AIOP)

Instruments the person rather than the technology. Six domains, each running from a harm pole to a benefit pole, read against the person's own baseline.

The Glass Ledger
