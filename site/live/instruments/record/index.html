Record

The Glass Ledger

A tamper-evident decision journal. Any rewrite of the record becomes detectable by anyone, from any machine.

What it does

Every entry carries the hash of the entry before it and a signature, so any rewrite of the record is detectable without trusting the machine that wrote it. One writer, one file, no consensus protocol: not a blockchain. Self-custody of the record ends here. The Ledger is the one instrument in the estate that has been built and measured rather than argued; both running implementations are described under Implementation.

When it is used

Eighth, and continuously: the record is written before the system acts, not after.
