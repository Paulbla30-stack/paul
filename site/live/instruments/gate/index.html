Gate

The Readiness Gate

Ten observable pre-deployment checks and four red lines. The only instrument whose output can be: do not deploy.

What it does

The gate is applied to the receiving team before any system is chosen. It treats deployment as multiplicative rather than additive: a capable system landing in a team that cannot absorb it multiplies harm instead of adding capability. Ten checks are observable on the floor without new software; four red lines stop the deployment outright.

When it is used

First. Everything else in the sequence assumes the gate has been passed.

  • The 24-Hour Team — Core paper 1 of 3. Managing round-the-clock care as two teams joined by a handover produces a family of predictable failures; the unit is one organism, and its vitals can be read.
