Measure

The AI Interaction Outcome Profile (AIOP)

Instruments the person rather than the technology. Six domains, each running from a harm pole to a benefit pole, read against the person's own baseline.

What it does

Every control currently governing conversational AI in health and care assesses the technology; none instruments the person. The Profile is a longitudinal outcome instrument across six domains — displacement to supplementation, agency, epistemic stance, regulation in absence, human alliance, disclosure pattern — each bipolar, each read against the person's own baseline. Its construct validity is mapped against the human-factors literature in a separate note.

When it is used

Seventh. Over time, paired with the Screen: the Screen assesses at the gate, the Profile measures the trajectory.
