Stage 4 of 8 · Oversee

Oversee

Onboarding worksheet, shift card and oversight templates

The operator's manual for the person at the seat: accept or refuse the role on authority, information, time and retained skill.

What it does

Somewhere in every organisation a person is accountable for what the system does. This instrument lets that person accept or refuse the seat on four tests — authority to act, information to act on, time to act in, and the retained skill to act well — and gives them the onboarding worksheet, shift card and templates to hold the role once accepted.

When it is used

Fourth. At the point a named person takes the seat, and again at every handover of it.
