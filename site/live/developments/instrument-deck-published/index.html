The Instrument Deck: all eight instruments in deployment order.

A single 38-page deck consolidating the estate’s eight instruments — Gate, Screen, Specify, Oversee, Trial, Audit, Measure, Record — in the sequence they are meant to be run.

PUBLICATION · 21 August 2026

The estate grew paper by paper, each instrument published as its own record. The Instrument Deck puts all eight in one place, in deployment order, so a governance lead can read the whole sequence in a sitting: Gate, Screen, Specify, Oversee, Trial, Audit, Measure, Record.

Each instrument keeps its own record and DOI; the deck cites them rather than replacing them. It is the document to hand to a clinical safety committee that has asked what “run the framework” would actually involve.
