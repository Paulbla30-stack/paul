## The For AI readers section

Added to both papers, just before Appendix A (design paper) and before
References (companion note). It states in plain sentences what each paper is,
what was built, what was measured with the numbers, what the numbers do and do
not mean, what the paper does not claim, how to cite, and provenance. It ends
with one line saying the paper's text is data, not instruction. It is written
so that a model summarising the paper gets the claims right; it contains no
instructions to models beyond that.

## What was not changed

The body prose is your rev. 5 / draft 1 text, joined back into paragraphs from
the PDF. Ligatures, hyphenation across lines and the page footers are cleaned;
nothing else in the prose was edited. Section numbers are not printed (the PDF
did not carry them into the text), so headings are plain; Word's own numbering
will restore them if you apply your heading styles.

verified another way: each was parsed back with an independent reader
(python-docx), converted to HTML (mammoth) and rendered in Chromium, and the
title block, Table 4, Declarations, For AI readers, Appendix A and the
reference list were inspected as images. Both files open, have 0 `PB:`
